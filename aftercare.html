THE MEMORY DEPARTMENT / CONTINUITY OF CARE

Life after treatment.

What comes after is just as important.

The effects of treatment are often felt most profoundly in the ordinary moments of everyday life. Returning home, reconnecting with loved ones, and becoming familiar with a changed relationship to the past are all part of the experience. Here, our former patients reflect on what life has been like since leaving our care.

✳

01 / PATIENT PERSPECTIVES

In their own words.

No two patients experience treatment in quite the same way. For some, the changes are immediate. For others, their significance becomes apparent only after returning to familiar surroundings. We have invited a selection of our former patients to share their experiences in the months following treatment.

PATIENT NO. 001 MEMORY RESTORATION
“My grandmother passed away twelve years ago, and somewhere along the way I forgot what her voice sounded like. That was the hardest part for me. I can hear her now, as clearly as if she were in the next room. My sister says she remembers her voice differently, but I suppose we all remember things in our own way.”

— J. B.

PATIENT NO. 002 MEMORY REMEDIATION
“There was a particular street I avoided for almost eight years. I used to take the longer way home without even thinking about it. Last week, I walked down that street and stopped for a coffee. My husband asked me how it felt to finally go back. I had to ask him what he meant.”

— C. V. R.

PATIENT NO. 003 POST-TREATMENT CARE
“I was nervous about my first appointment, but the staff made everything feel remarkably ordinary. They knew how I liked my coffee and had already arranged a room overlooking the garden. I appreciated that they remembered the little things.”

02 / CONTINUING PATIENT CARE

Beyond your final appointment.

Leaving The Memory Department does not mark the end of our responsibility to you. We recognize that changes to personal recollection may influence familiar routines, relationships, and one's understanding of personal history. Our continuing care services are intended to help patients navigate these adjustments in the months and years following treatment.

Former patients have access to follow-up consultations, individualized aftercare recommendations, and our exclusive collection of residential and personal care experiences. Patients are encouraged to contact their assigned specialist should they encounter unfamiliar emotional responses or difficulties reconciling their recollections with those of others.

PRIVATE CONSULTATIONS / PATIENT SERVICES

Let us begin with you.

If you are considering treatment, or would simply like to understand what life following a procedure might look like, our specialists are available to discuss your circumstances and address any questions you may have. An initial consultation carries no obligation to proceed.
